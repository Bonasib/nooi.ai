# Acceptable Use Policy

_nooi.ai · Last updated: 2026-09-26_

## Zero tolerance

Any sexual or sexualized content involving minors is strictly forbidden. We block it, close the account and report it to the authorities.

## Not allowed

Pornographic or sexually explicit content; intimate images of anyone without consent; deepfakes of real people made without their consent or to deceive; impersonation; harassment, hate or threats; content that promotes violence or terrorism; illegal goods and activities; scams and misleading ads; election misinformation; malware and spam.

## Real people

Only create content showing a real person's face or voice if you have their consent. Label AI-generated content where the law or the platform you publish on requires it.

## Intellectual property

Do not use characters, music, logos or footage you don't have rights to.

## Platform abuse

Do not bypass safety filters, credit limits or security, or resell access without permission.

## Enforcement

We use automatic filters and human review. Breaking this policy can lead to removal of content, loss of credits, suspension or permanent closure.
