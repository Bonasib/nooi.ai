# Payments & Refund Policy

_nooi.ai · Last updated: 2026-09-26_

## 1. Prices and currency

Prices are shown in Saudi riyals (SAR) and other currencies where offered, including VAT where it applies. A tax invoice is issued for every payment.

## 2. Subscriptions

Plans are billed monthly or yearly in advance and renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel any time in Account; access continues to the end of the paid period. We remind you before each yearly renewal.

## 3. Credits

Plan credits refresh each billing period and do not roll over. Top-up credits stay valid for 12 months. Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred or resold.

## 4. Delivery

nooi.ai is a digital service. Credits and plan features are available immediately after a successful payment, and you receive a confirmation and invoice by email.

## 5. Refunds

Because credits are used digitally, payments are not refundable once credits are spent, except: unused top-ups bought in the last 14 days get a full refund; a first subscription payment is refundable within 7 days if less than 10% of its credits were used; duplicate charges and technical errors are always refunded.

## 6. Failed generations

If a render fails on our side, its credits are returned to your balance automatically.

## 7. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 14 business days, depending on your bank. Request one at {email}.

## 8. Failed payments

If a renewal fails we retry and keep your plan active for a 7-day grace period, then move you to the free plan.

## 9. Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge — we resolve most issues quickly. Accounts with fraudulent chargebacks may be suspended.

## 10. Payment security

Payments are processed by PCI-DSS certified providers with 3-D Secure. We never see or store your full card number.
